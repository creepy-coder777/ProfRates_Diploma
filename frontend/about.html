About Us

ProfRates is an independent platform designed to help students make informed academic decisions by providing transparent, student-driven ratings of university faculty. Our goal is to bridge the gap between course selection and real classroom experience, offering a reliable source of insight into teaching quality, subject expertise, and faculty engagement.

Our Mission

We believe that every student deserves access to clear, unbiased information when choosing their professors. ProfRates exists to empower students with the tools they need to evaluate faculty based on actual performance, not just reputation. By aggregating verified ratings and reviews, we aim to foster a more informed, accountable, and student-centered academic environment.

What We Offer

ProfRates provides a simple interface where students can:

  • Browse faculty profiles across engineering disciplines
  • View star-based ratings and written feedback from peers
  • Filter professors by department, rating, or name
  • Explore areas of expertise and teaching focus
  • Identify top-rated faculty based on student feedback

Each professor profile includes a summary of their specialization, rating, and a short description of their academic strengths. The platform is mobile-friendly and easy to navigate — designed to give students the information they need quickly and confidently.

Why ProfRates Matters

Course selection is one of the most impactful decisions students make each semester. Yet too often, students rely on incomplete or outdated information. ProfRates bridges that gap by creating a transparent, data-driven community for academic feedback and decision-making.

By Students, For Students

Profrates was created with a deep understanding of student needs. Every feature—from the logic behind filters to the layout of rating cards—has been designed to prioritize clarity, ease of use, and relevance. We continuously improve the platform based on user feedback, ensuring it evolves alongside the academic community it serves.

Commitment To Integrity

We take the authenticity of feedback seriously. All ratings are submitted by verified users, and our moderation system ensures that responses remain constructive and respectful. Our goal isn't to randomly rank professors, but to provide a meaningful picture of their teaching impact—based on consistent, thoughtful student opinions.
